## Why this notebook matters
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is the first and most important step in any data science project. It helps reveal structure, anomalies, and hidden assumptions before modeling.

## Core idea
EDA turns raw data into understanding. The goal is to see the story the data tells before fitting a model.

## Mathematical intuition
Patterns such as skewness, outliers, missing values, and correlations can strongly influence modeling decisions. For example, a skewed feature may require transformation, and missing data may require imputation strategies.

## Intuition
EDA is where data science becomes detective work. You look for clues, weird values, and relationships that will matter downstream.

## Practical workflow in the notebook
- summarize features
- visualize distributions and relationships
- detect anomalies and missing data
- derive hypotheses about patterns
- document insights before modeling

## Interview-ready explanation
"EDA is the process of understanding the data before modeling. It helps identify noise, outliers, patterns, and feature relevance, which can determine whether a model will succeed or fail."

## Common pitfalls
- jumping directly to modeling without inspecting the data
- overlooking missingness patterns
- treating every outlier as an error instead of a signal

## Takeaway
This notebook is a reminder: understanding the data is not optional, it is the foundation of machine learning.
