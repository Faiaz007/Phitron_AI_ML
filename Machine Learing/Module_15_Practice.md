## Core idea
Models become useful only when their predictions can be interpreted, validated, and improved.

## Mathematical intuition
Evaluation metrics anchor model quality. Depending on the task, a model may be judged by accuracy, precision, recall, F1, log loss, RMSE, or MAE.

## Intuition
Better models come from better understanding: more relevant features, proper validation, and consistent measurement.

- ignoring class distribution
