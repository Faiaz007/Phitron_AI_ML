# Descriptive Statistics and Distributions

## Why this notebook matters
This notebook is the foundation of data understanding. Before modeling, we need to summarize data, detect patterns, and identify whether the distribution is skewed, symmetric, heavy-tailed, or multimodal.

## Core idea
Statistics gives us a language for describing datasets. We learn about central tendency, spread, skewness, and probability distributions.

## Mathematical intuition
Some important quantities are:
- mean: average value
- median: middle value
- variance: average squared distance from mean
- standard deviation: square root of variance
- covariance and correlation: relationship between variables

Distributions such as normal, uniform, or exponential tell us how likely values are across the range.

## Intuition
Data science is not only about code; it is about meaning. A good model depends on understanding the data rather than blindly applying algorithms.

## Practical workflow in the notebook
- inspect data shape and summary stats
- visualize distribution (histograms, boxplots)
- compute mean, variance, skewness
- relate observations to real-world context

## Interview-ready explanation
"Descriptive statistics summarize and explain data before modeling. Understanding distribution, variance, and skewness helps choose the right algorithms and avoid false assumptions."

## Common pitfalls
- treating all distributions as normal
- ignoring outliers
- overlooking the scale of features

## Takeaway
This notebook teaches a critical habit: understand the data before training a model.
